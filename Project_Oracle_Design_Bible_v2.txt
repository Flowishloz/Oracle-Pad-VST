- Material: Machined aluminum with a circular (conic) brushed texture.
- Cap: A high-polish "Chrome Cap" center with a specular white dot at the 11 o'clock position.
- Indicator: An "Inset LED" (a small glowing dot of #00D1FF near the outer rim).
- Shadow: A 4px drop shadow at 90 degrees to make the knob feel like it's hovering off the metal plate.

B. Typography (INCREASED LEGIBILITY)
- Scale constraint: Parameter labels must be clearly legible; increase base size to 12pt-14pt minimum.
- Brand Header: Eurostile Next or Microgramma (Bold, Wide).
- Readouts: Dot-Matrix or VFD (Vacuum Fluorescent Display) styles.
- Labels: Helvetica Condensed (All Caps). Tracking: +15%.

C. Buttons & Toggles
- Style: Translucent "Ice-Blue" polycarbonate.
- State: Unlit = #1A2A40. Lit = #00D1FF with an outer glow (bloom).
- Click: 1px vertical translation on click to simulate physical travel.

[4. JUCE / C++ IMPLEMENTATION HINTS]
- Bounding Boxes: Ensure `setBounds()` logic inside `resized()` provides enough room for the scaled-up knobs and larger text without overlapping into the ultra-thin section banners.
- Metallic Gradient: `ColourGradient (Colours::white.withAlpha (0.2f), 0, 0, Colours::black.withAlpha (0.2f), 0, height, false);`
- Glowing Cyan (OEL Effect): Use `Glow` or `DropShadow` with #00D1FF at 0.8 opacity and a 7px blur for active waveforms.
- The "Pioneer" OEL Screen Gradient: `ColourGradient (Colour(0xff020a1a), 0, 0, Colour(0xff051635), 0, height, false);`

[5. CLAUDE EXECUTION COMMANDS]
1. Strict Adherence: "Only use the Cool Blue, Warm Cyan, and Cold Silver palette. Remove all Magenta/Pink/Red."
2. Proportions: "Ensure knobs are large, parameter text is highly legible (12-14pt+), and section header banners are thin/sleek."
3. Skeuomorphism: "Every UI element must have a multi-layered shadow and a highlight. Emphasize high-detail bevels."
